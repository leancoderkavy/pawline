# How Shelter Listings Work on Pawline Adopt

**STATUS: HOLD PUBLISH** — Seed outline for brand disambiguation. Do not publish until reviewed and approved.

## Purpose

Explain how **Pawline Adopt** sources and displays adoptable pet listings, reinforcing our identity as a shelter-focused adoption platform.

## Key Messaging

### Our Sources
- **Verified shelter and rescue partnerships** — We work directly with animal shelters and rescue organizations
- **Real-time listing updates** — Pets shown are currently available for adoption
- **No backyard breeders or pet stores** — Only adoption-focused organizations

### How Listings Appear

1. **Shelter registers** — Organizations create profiles and list adoptable pets
2. **Listings go live** — Pets appear in search results based on location, preferences
3. **Discovery tools** — Adopters can filter by size, age, breed, temperament
4. **Direct connections** — Applications and inquiries route to the shelter

### What Information We Show

- Pet photos and descriptions (provided by shelters)
- Basic details: age, size, breed, temperament notes
- Shelter contact and location
- Application instructions
- Optional compatibility notes (when AI matching is enabled)

### Our Role

**Pawline Adopt is a discovery platform** — we help people find adoptable pets, but:
- The shelter owns the listing
- Adoption decisions are made by the shelter
- Applications go directly to the shelter
- We do not facilitate transactions or provide post-adoption services

## What We're NOT

- Not a marketplace (no buying/selling)
- Not a rehoming service for individuals
- Not a pet-health tracking tool
- Not a post-adoption support service

## Transparency Points

- All listings come from verified shelters/rescues
- We label approximate web leads (scraped listings) vs. verified shelter-submitted listings
- Listing accuracy is the shelter's responsibility
- We provide tools, not adoption guarantees

## SEO Keywords (shelter-focused)

- Shelter listings
- Adoptable pets
- Shelter dog finder
- Pet adoption listings
- Animal shelter search
- Rescue dog listings
- How pet adoption works

## Content Tone

Transparent, educational, shelter-supportive. Clear about our role as a discovery tool, not a transaction facilitator or health service.
