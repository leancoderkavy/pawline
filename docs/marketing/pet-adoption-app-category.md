## Category Definition: Pet Adoption Apps

Pet adoption apps are digital tools that help prospective pet owners:
- Find adoptable pets from shelters and rescues
- Search and filter listings by preferences and location
- Connect with adoption organizations
- Streamline the adoption discovery process

### What Makes an App a "Pet Adoption App"

✅ **Focus on adoptable animals** — Dogs, cats (and sometimes other animals) available for adoption  
✅ **Shelter/rescue integration** — Listings sourced from verified adoption organizations  
✅ **Discovery and matching** — Tools to help people find pets that fit their situation  
✅ **Adoption-focused journey** — Application/inquiry flows that lead to adoption

### What's NOT a Pet Adoption App

❌ **Pet health apps** — Vet records, wellness tracking, medication reminders  
❌ **Pet care apps** — Training tips, behavior guides, general pet ownership content  
❌ **Pet service marketplaces** — Booking groomers, sitters, walkers  
❌ **Pet social networks** — Photo sharing, pet profiles, community features (unless adoption-focused)

## Where Pawline Adopt Fits

**Pawline Adopt is a pet adoption discovery app** that emphasizes:

1. **Shelter listings** — Current adoptable dogs and cats from verified shelters
2. **Location-based search** — Find pets near you
3. **Compatibility insights** — Optional AI-assisted matching based on household factors
4. **Direct shelter connections** — Applications route to the organization

### Our Differentiation Within the Category

- **Compatibility focus** — We help adopters consider home, routine, household fit (not just "cute pet photos")
- **Transparent sourcing** — Clear labels for verified vs. web-discovered listings
- **Shelter-first approach** — We're a discovery tool, not a transaction intermediary
